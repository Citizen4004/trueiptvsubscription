Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Why We Don't Charge Upfront

Payment comes last here, not first. We get your stream running and confirmed over WhatsApp before any charge happens, which means the setup or compatibility problems that would normally justify a refund request tend to get caught and fixed before money is ever involved.

Once You've Paid

Because payment only happens after you've already seen the service working, we treat it as generally non-refundable from that point on. The exception is a real, ongoing outage that has nothing to do with your own device or internet connection, that's on us to sort out, so message support on WhatsApp and we'll find a fix or a fair adjustment.

Cancelling

Future renewals can be cancelled whenever you like. That doesn't reach backward to refund time you've already paid for and used.

If Something's Wrong

Message support first, on WhatsApp at +1 (209) 755-9199, before assuming a refund is the answer. The overwhelming majority of issues get sorted out through troubleshooting alone.

Contact

For anything not covered here, email contact@trueiptvsubscription.com.
