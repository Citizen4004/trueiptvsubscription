Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

True IPTV Subscription: review this page and adjust the specifics to match your actual policy before relying on it. This is a reasonable starting draft built around the "test before you pay" process, not a legal document.

Our Setup-First Approach

We don't collect payment up front. Your stream is set up and tested over WhatsApp first, and you only pay once you've confirmed it's working on your device. Because of this, most setup or compatibility problems get resolved before any money changes hands.

After Payment

Since payment only happens after you've confirmed the service works, subscriptions are generally non-refundable once payment is made and verified. If you hit a real, ongoing outage that isn't caused by your device or internet, contact our support team on WhatsApp and we'll work with you on a fix or an appropriate adjustment.

Cancellations

You can cancel future renewals at any time. Cancelling doesn't retroactively refund time you've already paid for.

Getting Support

If something isn't working the way it should, message us first on WhatsApp at +1 (209) 755-9199. Most issues get resolved quickly through troubleshooting.

Contact

For anything not covered here, email contact@trueiptvsubscription.com.
