Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how True IPTV Subscription ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit this website, message us on WhatsApp, or email us about our IPTV subscription service.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us directly, such as a phone number through WhatsApp or an email address, when you reach out about a subscription.
  • Setup information needed to get your service running, such as the device and player app you plan to use.
  • Basic technical data from visiting this site (browser type, for example), collected only through standard web server logs. We don't run advertising tracking pixels here.

How We Use It

  • To set up, activate, and support your subscription.
  • To communicate about your account, billing, or a technical issue.
  • To respond to anything you send us by email or WhatsApp.

What We Don't Do With It

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't hand your contact details to advertisers.

WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices, on top of this policy. We use it strictly for setup help and support.

How Long We Keep It

Account and support records are kept only as long as needed to deliver the service and resolve related support requests.

Your Options

You can ask us to delete your contact information at any time by emailing contact@trueiptvsubscription.com, unless we're required to keep certain records for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@trueiptvsubscription.com or WhatsApp at +1 (209) 755-9199.
