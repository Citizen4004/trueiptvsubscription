Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers what True IPTV Subscription ("we", "us", "our") does with your information across three places: this website, WhatsApp messages, and email.

What We Collect

  • Whatever you give us to reach you, a phone number if you message on WhatsApp, or an email address if that's how you contacted us about subscribing.
  • Details about your setup, specifically which device and player app you're using, since we need that to get your subscription working.
  • Standard web server logs from browsing this site, nothing beyond ordinary technical data like browser type.
  • Google Analytics data, using cookies to tell us which pages get traffic and how visitors arrived. No advertising pixels, and this data is never sold, it exists purely to understand site traffic.

What It's Used For

  • Getting your subscription set up, activated, and supported.
  • Reaching you about your account, a bill, or a technical problem.
  • Replying to whatever you send us by email or WhatsApp.

What We Won't Do With It

Your personal information isn't for sale, and your contact details never go to advertisers.

WhatsApp Specifically

Messaging us on WhatsApp means that conversation also falls under WhatsApp's own privacy practices, separate from and in addition to this policy. On our side, it's used only for setup help and support, nothing else.

Retention

We hold onto account and support records for only as long as it takes to deliver the service and close out any related support requests.

Your Choices

Email contact@trueiptvsubscription.com anytime to have your contact information deleted, except where we're required to keep specific records for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@trueiptvsubscription.com or WhatsApp at +1 (209) 755-9199.
