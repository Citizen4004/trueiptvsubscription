IPTV Subscription FAQ

Everything people usually ask before trying True IPTV Subscription. If yours isn't here, message us directly and we'll answer it ourselves.

About the service

What is an IPTV subscription?

An IPTV subscription gives you access to live TV channels delivered over the internet instead of cable or satellite, played through an app on a device you already own, like a Firestick, Smart TV, or phone.

Is True IPTV Subscription a legitimate provider?

Yes. We focus on a curated lineup of channels we're authorized to distribute, rather than reselling unauthorized global content. We also don't ask you to pay before your stream is tested and confirmed working, which is one of the simplest ways to tell a legitimate provider apart from a risky one.

What's included in a plan?

Every plan includes live channels across Sports, News, Kids and Local, a curated Movies & TV Shows library, 4K and Full HD streaming quality, a full program guide (EPG), and 24/7 WhatsApp support. The feature set doesn't change between plans, only the term length and device count do.

Trial & getting started

Does True IPTV Subscription offer a free trial?

Yes, a free 24-hour trial. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll set it up on your own device so you can check the channels and streaming quality before choosing a plan.

Do I have to pay before my subscription is set up?

No. We set up and test your stream over WhatsApp first, using Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist in your preferred player app. You only pay once you've confirmed everything works.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp, pick a plan or ask for the free trial, and a real support agent walks you through installing a player app and testing your stream.

Devices & usage

How many devices can one subscription cover?

1 to 5 simultaneous devices on every plan, selected when you choose a term on the plans page. Need more than 5? Message us for a custom quote.

What devices work with an IPTV subscription?

Any device that can run a player app: Firestick, Android TV boxes, Samsung/LG Smart TVs, Apple TV, iPhone/iPad, PC/Mac, Roku, and MAG/Formuler boxes. See our full device compatibility guide for setup specifics.

Do I need a VPN to use an IPTV subscription?

Not with a legitimate service, there's nothing to hide. The only real reason to add one is if your ISP is specifically throttling streaming traffic, which a VPN can sometimes work around.

Pricing & billing

How much does an IPTV subscription cost?

Plans start at $16.99 for 1 month, with better per-month value on longer terms, up to $79.99 for 12 months. See the full breakdown on the plans page.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, at any time. Plans here are single payments rather than recurring billing, so your access simply continues until the end of the term you already paid for.

Can I switch to a different term length later?

Yes. Message us before your current term ends and we'll apply a new term length starting from your renewal date.
