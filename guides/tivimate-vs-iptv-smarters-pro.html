TiviMate vs IPTV Smarters Pro: Which Should You Choose?

Both apps do the same basic job: they take your subscription and turn it into live channels, a program guide, and a way to browse your lineup. How they go about it, and where they'll actually run, is where the real differences show up.

What these two apps actually are

TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro are both third-party player apps built to work with any IPTV subscription that gives you Xtream Codes login details or an M3U playlist link. Neither one streams channels on its own. You add your subscription to whichever app you prefer, and the app handles the interface: the guide, the channel list, favorites, and playback controls. Because they both read the same kind of credentials, switching between them later, or running both side by side, doesn't require anything new from your provider.

Where they split apart is in design philosophy. TiviMate was built almost entirely around the living room experience, with a layout that assumes you're holding a remote and sitting a few feet from the screen. IPTV Smarters Pro was built to be the same app everywhere, from a TV box down to a phone in your pocket, which shapes a lot of the trade-offs below.

Platform support: where each one actually runs

This is usually the deciding factor before anything else gets considered. TiviMate is built on Android, so it runs on Android TV boxes, Fire TV and Firestick devices, and Android phones and tablets. It has no official iOS app and no native version for Samsung Tizen or LG webOS smart TVs, since those platforms don't run Android apps at all.

IPTV Smarters Pro covers a lot more ground. Alongside Android and Fire TV, it has a proper iOS app for iPhone and iPad, plus dedicated apps for Windows and Mac. If your household mixes an Android box in the living room with an iPhone for watching in bed, Smarters Pro is the one app that works identically across all of it.

The program guide experience

TiviMate's EPG is the app's calling card. It's a full-screen, grid-style guide that scrolls smoothly with a remote, shows a live preview of whatever's currently playing in a small window while you browse, and generally feels closer to a cable or satellite box than a typical streaming app menu. For anyone who spends a lot of time just flipping through what's on, this is the stronger experience of the two by a clear margin.

IPTV Smarters Pro has a functional guide too, with channel listings, a timeline view, and catch-up support where your provider offers it, but the layout is more basic and the navigation isn't quite as fluid with a remote. It's perfectly usable, especially on a phone or tablet where you're tapping instead of scrolling with a directional pad, but it doesn't try to replicate the cable-box feel the way TiviMate does.

Premium and paid features

IPTV Smarters Pro is free with no in-app purchase tier. Every feature in the app is available to every user, which keeps things simple but also means there's no extra layer to unlock later.

TiviMate's core playback, guide, and favorites are also free, but it offers an optional Premium upgrade for people who want more. Premium adds multi-screen viewing so you can watch more than one channel at once, a PIN-protected parental lock, custom channel logos and theme colors, and cloud backup of your settings so switching devices doesn't mean rebuilding everything from scratch. None of it is necessary for basic use, but it's worth knowing the option exists if one of those specific features appeals to you.

Which devices fit best

If your entire setup lives on one Firestick or Android TV box in the living room, either app will serve you well, and the choice mostly comes down to whether you prefer TiviMate's guide or IPTV Smarters Pro's simplicity. The picture changes once more than one kind of device is involved.

A household with an iPhone, a Windows laptop, and a Fire TV box all pulling from the same subscription is the clearest case for IPTV Smarters Pro, since it's the only one of the two that installs cleanly on all three. On the other hand, a setup that's purely Android TV boxes and Firesticks across multiple rooms plays to TiviMate's strengths, particularly its support for multiple profiles so each person can keep their own favorites on a shared device.

Everyday extras worth knowing about

TiviMate supports adding more than one playlist to a single install, useful if you ever combine subscriptions, and its profile system lets different family members keep separate favorites and channel ordering without interfering with each other. IPTV Smarters Pro keeps things a bit more straightforward, with one main profile per install, catch-up and recording support depending on what your provider sends through, and a built-in option to multi-screen a few channels at once on supported devices without needing a paid upgrade.

Both apps sync your channel list and guide data automatically once your subscription is added, and neither one works offline, since live TV has to stream in real time either way.

So which one should you actually install?

If you're watching mainly on a Firestick or Android TV box and want the guide to feel as close to a real cable box as possible, go with TiviMate. If you need the same app and the same login working across a phone, a laptop, and a TV box without hunting for separate installs, IPTV Smarters Pro is the simpler path. Plenty of people end up installing both and just using whichever fits the device in front of them, since both read the same subscription details without any conflict.

Frequently asked questions

Is TiviMate better than IPTV Smarters Pro?

Neither is flatly better. TiviMate has the stronger program guide and feels more like a cable box, while IPTV Smarters Pro covers far more devices including iPhone, iPad, Windows, and Mac.

Can I use both apps with the same subscription?

Yes. Both apps connect to your subscription using the same Xtream Codes details or M3U playlist URL, so you can install either one, or both, without needing separate credentials.

Does IPTV Smarters Pro work on iPhone?

Yes, IPTV Smarters Pro has a dedicated iOS app for iPhone and iPad. TiviMate does not have an iOS version.

Which app has a better program guide?

TiviMate's EPG is generally considered the stronger of the two, with a full-screen grid layout, live channel previews, and smoother remote navigation.

Do I have to pay extra for either app?

IPTV Smarters Pro is free with no paid tier. TiviMate is free for core playback, with an optional Premium upgrade for multi-screen viewing, a parental PIN, and cloud backup.

Which one should I install on Firestick?

Either works well on Firestick. TiviMate tends to feel more polished for daily live TV browsing, while IPTV Smarters Pro is a solid, simple option if you also want the same look on a phone or laptop.
