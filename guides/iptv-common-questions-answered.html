IPTV Questions, Answered

A reference page for the questions people ask before, during, and after signing up for IPTV. Short answers, organized by topic, so you can scan for what you need.

Getting started

What is IPTV, in plain terms?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. Instead of channels arriving through a cable line or a satellite dish, they're delivered over your regular internet connection. You watch through an app on a device like a Firestick, phone, or Smart TV, rather than a cable box. The channel itself looks the same once it's playing, the delivery method underneath is just different.

What devices can I use?

Firestick and Fire TV are the most common choice because they're cheap and widely available. Android TV boxes, Android phones and tablets work too. Most modern Smart TVs can run a compatible app directly, and if your TV can't, a small streaming box plugged into an HDMI port solves that instantly. Computers work through a web player or desktop app like VLC. Our devices page breaks down setup by hardware if you're deciding what to buy.

Do I need special equipment?

No. You need a device capable of running an app and a stable internet connection, that's genuinely it. No satellite dish, no specific router, no extra box beyond whatever you're already using to stream other apps like Netflix or YouTube.

Is it legal?

IPTV as a technology is a completely standard way of delivering television over the internet. Plenty of major telecom and cable companies use IPTV infrastructure for their own official services. What matters is the content being delivered and whether the provider has the rights to distribute it. We operate as a legitimate subscription business and don't claim to be affiliated with any specific channel's broadcaster. If this is a concern for you, our FAQ page covers it in more detail.

Channels and content

How many channels do I get?

It varies by plan, but most plans include several thousand live channels spanning a wide mix of countries, sports, news, and entertainment categories, on top of a library of movies and TV shows available on demand. The exact count depends on which plan you choose.

Are local channels included?

Usually yes, though availability depends on your region and which specific channels you're after. If local channels matter to you, it's worth asking before you commit to a plan length. Our guide on how local channels work with IPTV goes into more depth.

Can I pause or rewind live TV?

That depends on the app you use. Some players support catch-up or rewind on certain channels when the provider sends that data along with the stream, others only support straight live playback. It's app-specific rather than a guarantee across the board.

Is there an on-demand library?

Yes, most plans include a Movies and TV Shows section separate from the live channel list, browsable by genre or search, working more like a traditional streaming catalog.

Devices and streams

How many devices can I use at once?

Your plan sets a connection limit, the number of streams that can run at the exact same time. You can install the app on every device in your house if you want, the limit only applies to simultaneous playback, not installations. A two-connection plan means two screens can play different channels at once, a third attempt gets blocked until one of the first two stops.

Can I switch devices without losing my subscription?

Yes. Your login isn't tied to a single piece of hardware. Install the app on a new device, enter the same details, and you're back where you left off.

What if my internet connection drops?

Live channels stream in real time, so a dropped connection stops playback until your internet comes back. There's no offline mode for live TV since it isn't downloaded ahead of time the way a movie file would be.

Common problems

Why is my stream buffering?

Usually it traces back to your Wi-Fi signal, an overloaded router, or occasionally your internet provider slowing down streaming traffic during busy hours. Our buffering troubleshooting guide runs through the fixes in order of how often they actually solve it.

Why won't my app log in?

Usually a typo in the server address, username, or password, or credentials that were entered with extra spaces. Double-checking character by character fixes most login issues. Our login error guide covers the full checklist.

My program guide is empty. What's wrong?

Guide data usually just needs a minute to sync after you first add your playlist, or after a longer gap since you last opened the app. Give it a bit and refresh. Our dedicated EPG troubleshooting guide covers the less common causes too.

Frequently asked questions

What devices can I use IPTV on?

Firestick, Android TV boxes, Android phones and tablets, most Smart TVs, and computers through a web player or app like TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, or VLC.

How many channels do I get?

It depends on the plan, but most IPTV subscriptions, including ours, include several thousand live channels covering a wide range of countries and categories, plus movies and shows on demand.

How many devices can I use at once?

This is set by your plan's connection limit, commonly one, two, or more simultaneous streams. You can install the app on as many devices as you want, you just can't stream on more devices at the same time than your plan allows.

How does payment work?

You pick a plan length, pay through the checkout page, and receive your login details shortly after, usually by email or WhatsApp depending on what you select at checkout.

Do I need special equipment?

No special hardware. A device that can run an app, like a Firestick, Android box, or Smart TV, plus a stable internet connection, is all you need.

What happens if my internet goes down?

IPTV streams live over the internet, so if your connection drops, playback stops until it's back. There's no offline or downloaded viewing mode for live channels.
