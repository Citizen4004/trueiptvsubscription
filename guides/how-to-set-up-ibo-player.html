How to Set Up IBO Player

IBO Player gets picked a lot by people who want something that looks modern out of the box, no clunky menus, no dated icons, and it happens to run natively on Samsung and LG TVs as well as Android, Fire TV, iOS, and Windows. Here's how to get your subscription connected.

Step by step

  1. Install the app. On a Samsung TV, open the Smart Hub and search for "IBO Player." On LG, use the LG Content Store. On Android, Android TV, or Fire TV, install it from Google Play or the Amazon Appstore. On iPhone or iPad, use the App Store, and on Windows, grab the desktop build.
  2. Find the add playlist option. On first launch, or from the main settings menu afterward, look for an option to add a new playlist or source.
  3. Choose your login type. Pick Xtream Codes API if you have a server URL, username, and password, or M3U URL if you've been given a single playlist link.
  4. Enter your details carefully. Copy and paste where possible rather than typing on a remote, which cuts down on the typo issues that cause most failed logins.
  5. Add your EPG source. If the program guide doesn't show up automatically, look for a separate EPG field and paste in your XMLTV guide URL there.
  6. Save and let it load. IBO Player pulls in your channel list and guide data, and you're ready to start watching.

Why people switch to IBO Player

A lot of IPTV apps were built years ago and still look it, cluttered grids, tiny text, menus that feel like they were designed for a remote control from a different decade. IBO Player's draw is mostly about feel: smoother transitions, a cleaner channel list, and a layout that doesn't require squinting from the couch. If you've been using an older app and found yourself annoyed by how dated it feels rather than anything actually broken, IBO Player is a common upgrade path.

Native support on Samsung and LG

Plenty of popular IPTV apps, TiviMate among them, are built for Android and simply don't install on Samsung's Tizen or LG's webOS. IBO Player is one of the exceptions, it has versions built specifically for both, available straight from each TV's own app store. That means no Firestick, no separate Android box, just the TV itself running the app directly. If your household's main screen is a newer Samsung or LG set and you'd rather not add another box under the TV, this is one of the more straightforward options.

About that activation screen on first launch

Some versions of IBO Player show a short activation or trial step the first time you open the app, separate from anything related to your actual IPTV subscription. That screen belongs to the player software itself. It's easy to confuse with the playlist login step that comes right after, so if you hit a screen asking about activation before you've even seen an Xtream Codes or M3U field, that's normal, just follow whatever it asks and continue on to adding your playlist afterward.

Keeping your guide data current

IBO Player pulls EPG data either bundled with your Xtream Codes login or from a separate XMLTV link you paste in during setup. If your channels play fine but the schedule grid is blank, a stale EPG link is almost always the reason, double-check that the URL hasn't expired or changed on your provider's end, and re-enter it if needed. Some installs benefit from a manual refresh under the app's settings menu rather than waiting for it to update on its own schedule.

Running IBO Player across several devices

Because IBO Player installs on such a wide spread of device types, it's common for one household to run it on a Samsung TV in the living room and a phone or Fire TV stick in a bedroom, all logged into the same account. That's fine on IBO Player's end, the one thing to keep in mind is that your IPTV plan itself has its own limit on how many screens can stream at the same time, which is separate from how many devices have the app installed.

IBO Player versus Smart IPTV on a Samsung or LG TV

If you're specifically comparing native Smart TV options, Smart IPTV is the other big name here, but it works differently: it activates by MAC address rather than taking an Xtream Codes or M3U login directly inside the app. IBO Player's setup is more familiar if you're used to typing credentials straight into the app, so which one fits better often comes down to whether you'd rather register a device once through a web page or just log in like you would anywhere else.

Keeping IBO Player updated and moving to a new device

Since IBO Player is distributed through several different stores depending on the platform, updates don't all arrive the same way. On Android and Fire TV, Google Play and the Amazon Appstore handle it automatically if auto-update is turned on. On Samsung and LG, updates come through the Smart Hub or LG Content Store, which some TVs only check periodically rather than instantly, so a manual check from the store's app page is worth doing if a feature mentioned in a newer guide doesn't match what you're seeing. On iOS, the App Store handles it the usual way, and on Windows, you'll want to check back on the download page for the desktop build occasionally since there's no automatic background updater built into most desktop IPTV apps.

Setting IBO Player up on a second or replacement device is quick once you've done it the first time: install the app fresh, go through the same Add Playlist step, and re-enter the same Xtream Codes or M3U details you used before. Nothing about your subscription changes when you move to a new TV or phone, you're just repeating the login step on a new installation, and your old device can keep working alongside the new one as long as you stay within your plan's connection limit.

Frequently asked questions

Does IBO Player support Xtream Codes?

Yes. IBO Player accepts Xtream Codes API logins as well as M3U playlist URLs when you add a new source.

Does IBO Player work on Samsung and LG TVs natively?

Yes, IBO Player installs directly from the Samsung Smart Hub and LG Content Store, so it runs on the TV itself without a separate Firestick or Android box.

What devices does IBO Player run on?

Samsung Smart TV, LG Smart TV, Android, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV, iOS, and Windows are all supported.

Why does my IBO Player guide look empty?

Check that an XMLTV EPG link has been entered under the app's EPG or guide settings, separate from the playlist itself.

I see an activation or license screen when I first open IBO Player, what is that?

That screen belongs to the player app itself, not your IPTV subscription. Follow whatever on-screen instructions the app gives for that step, then move on to adding your playlist separately.

Can I use the same IBO Player account on more than one TV?

You can install IBO Player on multiple devices and add the same Xtream Codes or M3U details to each, though your IPTV plan's own connection limit still applies.
