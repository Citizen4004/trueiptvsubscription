How to Set Up Hot Player

Hot Player is a lesser-known name next to TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro, but the setup process is just as straightforward: install it, add your subscription, and watch. Here's the full walkthrough for Android and Fire TV.

What you'll need

  • An Android phone, tablet, Android TV box, or Fire TV device
  • Your Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist link
  • A few minutes

Step by step

  1. Install Hot Player. On Android or Android TV, grab it from Google Play. On Fire TV, since it isn't in the Amazon Appstore, you'll sideload it the same way as other player apps, using the Downloader app and the official download link.
  2. Open the add-playlist screen. Launch Hot Player and look for the option to add a new playlist or subscription.
  3. Choose your login type. Select Xtream Codes if you have a server URL, username, and password, or M3U if you have a single playlist link.
  4. Enter your details. Type them in exactly as given, then confirm.
  5. Let it load. Hot Player pulls in your channel list. Once that's done, you're ready to watch.

Xtream Codes or M3U, which keeps things more organized?

Like most player apps, Hot Player handles both delivery methods, but Xtream Codes tends to keep a Movies & TV Shows library sorted into proper categories, while a plain M3U link sometimes dumps everything into one long list depending on how the playlist itself is structured. If your subscription includes on-demand content and you have a choice of which login method to use, Xtream Codes is generally the tidier option.

Hot Player is Android and Fire TV only

There is no iOS version of Hot Player, and none is listed for iPad either. If anyone in your household watches on an iPhone or iPad, they'll need a separate app for that device, GSE Smart IPTV or IPTV Smarters Pro both cover iOS and work with the same Xtream Codes or M3U login you'd use in Hot Player. On Android phones, Android tablets, Android TV boxes, and Fire TV, Hot Player runs the same app with the same menus, so once you've set it up on one of those you already know the layout on the others.

Sideloading Hot Player onto Fire TV

Since Hot Player isn't listed in the Amazon Appstore, getting it onto a Fire TV device follows the same sideloading process used for most smaller IPTV apps: enable Apps from Unknown Sources under Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options, install the free Downloader app from the Appstore, then use Downloader to fetch Hot Player's installer from its official download page. It's the identical process covered in our Firestick installation guide, just pointed at a different app's download link. On Android TV boxes with Google Play access, you can skip sideloading entirely and install it straight from the Play Store.

Keeping the app updated

Because Hot Player on Fire TV is sideloaded rather than installed through the Appstore, it won't update itself automatically the way Appstore apps do. When a new version comes out, open Downloader again and enter the same download URL you used originally, it installs over the existing app as an update rather than creating a duplicate. On Android through Google Play, updates happen automatically as long as that's enabled on your device, so this mainly applies to Fire TV installs specifically.

Switching away from Hot Player later

If you start on Hot Player and later decide you want a more feature-rich app, your Xtream Codes or M3U credentials aren't locked into it. The same login details work in TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, or any other standard player app, so trying Hot Player first and switching later costs nothing beyond the few minutes it takes to set up the new app.

Frequently asked questions

What devices does Hot Player support?

Android phones and tablets, plus Fire TV and Android TV boxes.

Does Hot Player support Xtream Codes?

Yes, alongside M3U playlist URLs.

Is Hot Player available on iOS?

No. Hot Player is Android and Fire TV only, so iPhone and iPad users should use an app like GSE Smart IPTV instead.

Does Hot Player include a program guide?

Yes, a built-in EPG is included and populates automatically once your subscription's channel list loads.

Can I use Hot Player on more than one device?

You can install Hot Player on every Android or Fire TV device you own and log in with the same details each time. How many of them can stream at the same moment depends on which plan you picked, see our plans page for the device counts on each.
