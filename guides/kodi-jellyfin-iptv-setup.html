Using Kodi or Jellyfin for IPTV

If your movies and shows already live in Kodi or Jellyfin, you don't need a separate app just for live channels, both can take an IPTV playlist directly. Plex is the exception here, and it's worth knowing why before you waste an evening trying to make it work.

Setting up Kodi

Kodi's live TV support comes from an addon called PVR IPTV Simple Client. It ships with Kodi already but sits switched off until you turn it on.

  1. Open Settings → Add-ons → My add-ons → PVR clients and select PVR IPTV Simple Client.
  2. Toggle it on, then tap its configuration icon.
  3. On the General tab, paste your M3U playlist URL into the M3U Play List URL field.
  4. On the EPG Settings tab, add your XMLTV URL so the program guide populates.
  5. Confirm and restart Kodi. Open the TV tab and channels should load within a minute or so.

Nothing showing up after restarting is almost always a copy-paste issue with the playlist URL, check that the whole link made it in without getting cut off.

Setting up Jellyfin

Jellyfin builds Live TV directly into its dashboard, so there's no addon to track down first.

  1. Open the Jellyfin dashboard and click Live TV in the sidebar.
  2. Under Tuner Devices, add a new tuner and select M3U Tuner.
  3. Paste in your playlist URL.
  4. Under TV Guide Data Providers, add your XMLTV URL for program listings.
  5. Give it a minute or two to scan and import, larger channel lists take a bit longer the first time.

Once it's done, live channels show up alongside your existing movie and show library instead of in a separate app.

Why Plex is the odd one out

Plex built its Live TV & DVR feature around physical HDHomeRun tuner hardware picking up broadcast signals, not around internet playlists. There's no setting inside Plex where an M3U URL or an Xtream Codes login goes, the way there is in Kodi or Jellyfin. A few unofficial community tools exist that create a virtual tuner Plex can see and feed your playlist through it, but that's another piece of software on top of your subscription to install, configure, and keep updated, and it isn't something our support team can troubleshoot for you. If staying inside one unified library matters to you the way it does with Plex, Jellyfin gets you the closest to that experience with IPTV actually supported out of the box.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I just paste my M3U link into Plex?

Plex's Live TV & DVR feature is designed around physical HDHomeRun tuner hardware, not internet M3U or Xtream Codes playlists, so there's no official field anywhere in Plex for that kind of link. Unofficial third-party tools can bridge a playlist into something Plex's tuner system will accept, but that's extra software to install and maintain, not something built into Plex itself.

What's the simplest IPTV option for someone who uses Plex for movies?

Jellyfin, since it's a similar unified media-library experience but built with IPTV support natively, no bridge software required.

Do I need the EPG link, or does the playlist alone work?

The playlist alone gets channels playing. The XMLTV guide URL is what fills in program names and times. Both Kodi and Jellyfin work fine without it, you'll just have channels with no schedule data.
