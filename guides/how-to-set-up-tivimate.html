How to Set Up TiviMate

TiviMate is a favorite for its remote-friendly program guide, the kind that feels more like a cable box than a typical app menu. Getting your subscription loaded into it takes about two minutes.

Step by step

  1. Install the app. On Firestick, search the Amazon Appstore for "TiviMate." On Android or Android TV, grab it from Google Play.
  2. Open the playlist screen. First time opening TiviMate, it prompts you to add a playlist right away. Used it before? Go to Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist.
  3. Pick your login type. Choose Xtream Codes Login if you've got a server URL, username, and password, or M3U Playlist for a single link.
  4. Type in your details. Enter them exactly as provided, a stray space or typo is the most common reason this step fails.
  5. Wait for sync. TiviMate pulls your channels and guide. Once it's done, you're browsing from the main screen.

Is Premium worth paying for?

The free tier already covers full live playback, the program guide, and basic favorites, genuinely enough for most people. Premium unlocks a handful of specific extras: multi-screen viewing so you can watch more than one channel at a time, a PIN-protected parental lock, custom channel logos and colors, and cloud backup so a new device doesn't mean rebuilding your setup from scratch. It's worth the upgrade mainly if one of those specific features matters to you, not as a blanket "better version" of the app.

Favorites and multiple profiles

Press and hold on any channel in the guide to add it to Favorites, a shortcut tab worth setting up once your channel list gets long enough to make scrolling annoying. Separately, under Settings → Profiles, TiviMate lets different people in the house keep their own favorites and channel order without stepping on each other's setup, all while pulling from the same underlying subscription.

A few shortcuts worth knowing

Know a channel's number? Type it on your remote to jump straight there instead of scrolling. Press and hold on a channel in the guide for quick info and options without leaving the screen. And while you're browsing ahead in the guide, TiviMate keeps the currently playing channel running in a small preview window rather than freezing on a static list, so you don't lose track of what's actually on.

Frequently asked questions

Does TiviMate support Xtream Codes?

Yes, alongside M3U playlist URLs, both are available when you add a new playlist.

Is TiviMate free to use?

The free version covers live playback, the program guide, and favorites. A paid Premium tier adds multi-screen viewing, a parental PIN lock, custom channel art, and cloud backup of your settings.

Why is my program guide empty in TiviMate?

Usually the guide data just hasn't synced yet. Refresh the playlist under Settings → Playlists, or wait a few minutes.

Can each person in my household have their own favorites?

Yes, TiviMate supports multiple profiles on one installation, each with separate favorites and channel ordering, sharing the same subscription.
