IPTV Not Working? Answer This First

"It's not working" could mean five different things, and each one has a different fix. Rather than working through every possible cause, find the description below that matches what you're actually seeing and skip straight to the real fix.

First, rule out your connection

Before anything else, run a speed test on the same device that's having trouble, not a different one. If the result is well below what you normally get, or other apps and websites are also crawling, that's your internet, not IPTV. Unplug your router for 30 seconds, plug it back in, let it fully reconnect, then close and reopen the app. That one combination clears up a large share of reported problems on its own.

"The picture keeps freezing or breaking up"

Channel loads, but playback stutters or pixelates. That's a bandwidth or network-stability symptom almost every time. We've laid out the full diagnostic, including the specific decode-mode and wifi-band settings that tend to cause it, in our buffering fix guide.

"My channel list is empty or won't load at all"

This is rarely a dead account. App cache, a stale login, or an outdated app version cause this far more often than an actual subscription problem. See TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro not loading channels for the exact steps for both apps.

"It says my login or credentials are wrong"

A separate issue from an empty channel list, with its own cause: usually a typo, an expired credential, or a brief server-side hiccup. See IPTV login error or invalid credentials to narrow down which one applies to you.

"Channels play, but the guide is blank or wrong"

Playback working while the on-screen guide is empty or shows incorrect times is its own, smaller category of problem, almost always a sync issue with the guide data specifically. Our program guide troubleshooting page covers the fix, including what's different in TiviMate versus IPTV Smarters Pro.

"Nothing changed on my end, but it stopped working anyway"

When you genuinely haven't changed anything, the cause usually sits outside your control and typically resolves on its own. A short provider-side maintenance window can briefly interrupt streams. An automatic app or firmware update, especially on MAG and Formuler boxes, can quietly reset a setting overnight. ISPs occasionally throttle streaming-type traffic during busy evening hours even while your general connection tests fine. Give it 15 to 20 minutes before assuming anything needs fixing.

"It's only on wifi" or "it's only on one device"

The pattern itself tells you where to look. Works on ethernet but not wifi: that's your router, not your subscription, try the other frequency band (2.4GHz reaches further but gets crowded, 5GHz is faster but drops off with distance), move closer to the router, and check whether a VPN is running, since some configurations interfere with streaming specifically. Works on your phone but not your TV box: that's local to that device's app, clear its cache, confirm it's updated, and if it's a Firestick or Android box, check it isn't nearly out of storage, a cramped device behaves exactly like this.

Matched your symptom above and still stuck? Message our support team on WhatsApp with your exact device, app, and what you've already tried. The more specific the report, the faster we can actually help.

When to stop troubleshooting and just ask

If the problem is limited to one specific channel while everything else is fine, that's a provider-side feed issue to report, not something to keep restarting devices over. And once you've already done the restart-and-cache-clear basics with no change after 15 minutes or so, further guessing rarely helps, a support agent checking your account and the server side directly will solve it faster than continuing to poke at settings.

What to send support so they don't have to ask

A report that says "it's broken" gets a round of basic questions back before anyone can actually help. Save a step by including these up front: your exact device and app (for example, "Firestick, TiviMate" or "Samsung Smart TV, built-in app"), which channels or channel categories are affected versus which still work fine, whether it happens on wifi, ethernet, or both, and what you've already tried. A message with those four details usually gets a specific, useful answer on the very first reply instead of a slow back-and-forth that just repeats the basics you already covered.

A note on device age and storage

Older streaming boxes and budget Firestick models run into a specific pattern worth knowing about: as internal storage fills up with cached app data over months of use, playback apps in general, not just IPTV ones, start behaving erratically before anything actually fails outright. Channels load slowly, apps crash on launch, or playback stutters even on a fast connection. If your device is more than a couple of years old and none of the fixes above help, checking available storage and clearing out unused apps is worth doing before assuming the subscription itself is the problem.

Frequently asked questions

My IPTV worked fine yesterday and now it doesn't. What changed?

Often nothing you did. A brief provider-side maintenance window, an ISP hiccup, or an overnight app update resetting a setting are the three most common causes when nothing on your end actually changed. Give it 15 to 20 minutes and retry before assuming it's serious.

How do I tell if the problem is my internet or the subscription?

Run a speed test on the exact device giving you trouble. Normal speed but IPTV still won't load points to the app or the subscription. Noticeably slow speed, or other sites being sluggish too, points to your connection.

It only happens on wifi, not on ethernet. Is my subscription broken?

No, that pattern specifically points at your wifi setup, since a wired connection skips wifi entirely and still works fine. Try the other frequency band, move closer to the router, and check if a VPN is active.

One channel is broken but everything else plays fine. What's wrong?

This is almost always a single feed issue on the provider side, not your device or account. Message support with the exact channel name so it can be checked and reported upstream.

I've tried restarting everything and it's still broken. What now?

Stop guessing and message support directly, with your device, your app, and exactly what you've already tried. That gets you a targeted answer instead of repeating basic steps that already didn't work.
