How buying works

There's no checkout page where you pay first and hope it works. The process is:

  1. Pick a plan above, 1, 3, 6, or 12 months, and your device count.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp. A real support agent replies to set things up.
  3. We install and test your stream on your actual device using Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist.
  4. You pay only once it's confirmed working.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the per-month cost drop on longer plans?

Every plan is a single payment for the whole term, not a monthly charge, so a longer commitment lowers the effective monthly cost: $16.99/mo on the 1-month plan down to around $6.67/mo on the 12-month plan.

Is this a recurring subscription?

No. Each plan is a one-time payment for the term you pick, with no auto-renewal. You can cancel at any time, and access continues until the term you paid for ends.

Do all four plans include the same channels?

Yes. The 1, 3, 6, and 12 month plans all include identical live channels, the Movies & TV Shows library, 4K/Full HD quality, and the program guide. Only the term length and price change.

How does device pricing work?

Every plan supports 1 to 5 simultaneous devices, selectable right on this page, with a better per-device rate the more you add. Message us for a custom quote above 5 devices.

Do I pay before or after my stream is set up?

After. We set up and test your stream on WhatsApp first, using Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist, and you only pay once it's confirmed working.

Can I get a refund?

Since you confirm the service works before paying, subscriptions are generally non-refundable afterward. If a real, ongoing issue comes up that isn't on your end, message us and we'll work with you on a fix. See our refund policy for details.
